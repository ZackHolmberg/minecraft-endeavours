## R6 death at (-312,61,-580) (t2.coal)
- Cause: **drowning**. RCON: (-312,60..62,-580) is water sealed by sandstone (y=63) over sand (y=59), open only to a flooded tunnel; the bot (spawned at the lake bottom, y=52) swam in to reach coal at y=53. Telemetry: in the pocket at ...192, first `hurt` at ...199 (~15 s = oxygen 300 ticks), then 1-2 hp/s until death; no sand/gravel was involved. Probe reproduced it: 15 s underwater, then 2 hp/s "drowning".
- Also found: mineflayer 4.39 `entities.js` sets `bot.oxygenLevel` from EVERY entity's `air_supply` (fish/squid/other players), seen jumping 9->19->17. The reflex reads our own entity's packet instead (`auto-behaviors.ts:546` `trackSurvivalState`).
- Reflex `auto-behaviors.ts:339` `survivalTick` (400 ms reflex tick, preempts skills): oxygen <= 8 (`SURFACE_AT`), or falling <= 14 with air farther than oxygen*1.3 blocks / no open air -> `reflex: "surface"` event with reason, drops the pathfinder goal, holds jump and swims `findAirRoute` (`surfacing.ts:94`, BFS through water) to a head-in-air cell (`swimToAir:419`); sealed water -> `findRoofDig` (`surfacing.ts:117`) digs <= 3 natural blocks, never sand/gravel. Head inside a full solid cube 2 ticks -> `digOut:500` (dig if natural, else walk to the nearest free cell). `navigate` re-issues its goal after the reflex (`navigation.ts:270`, `surfaceInterrupted:308`, <= 3 resumes). followPlayer etc. are not resumed.
- Falling blocks: `pathfinder-config.ts:240` sets `dontMineUnderFallingBlock` (2.4.5 supports it, default true; now explicit) and adds red_sand, concrete powder, anvils, suspicious sand/gravel to `gravityBlocks`. Explicit digs: `fallsOnBot` (`structure-guard.ts:221`) refuses (skips as unreachable) a target with a falling block directly above while the bot stands in its column (`world.ts:571`); beside the column is allowed.
- Swimming: `LIQUID_COST` 3 -> 8 (`pathfinder-config.ts:49,234`; a swim step now costs more than digging rock), `allowFreeMotion=false` explicit (:237).
- Telemetry: `hurt` events carry `cause` (`inferHurtCause:567`: mob name, suffocation, drowning, lava, fire, fall, magma, cactus, starvation); `ReflexName` adds `surface`; dashboard/panel render it.
- Probes (scratch bot on the test server, deleted): open-ended pocket -> surfaced at o2 8, hp never below 18; sealed 9x2 water box under a 2-thick sandstone roof with a stone pickaxe -> dug through and breathed (o2 12 -> 20).
